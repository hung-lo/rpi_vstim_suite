# Pi 5 hardware acceptance status

The repository is an internal lab prototype, not production-certified. The
validated scope and the remaining apparatus/calibration work are summarized in
the [current Pi 5 deployment notes](pi5_rig.md) and the individual
[hardware records](hardware/).

Current evidence includes leased KMS natural-image presentation, dual-camera
recording, event/session integrity, direct NVMe storage, lick-input electrical
checks, reward-output line checks, Chrony/NeuroKairos IRIG-H operation, optical
IRIG recovery, standalone USB audio, and the approximately two-minute
[full-stack visual/USB-audio coexistence run](hardware/pi5_av_coexistence_2026-10-16.md).

Still pending are animal-facing water calibration, audio SPL and physical
acoustic-onset calibration, photodiode visual-onset calibration, and downstream
acquisition-device IRIG validation. GPIO line checks do not constitute a
validated fluidics or behavioral apparatus.
