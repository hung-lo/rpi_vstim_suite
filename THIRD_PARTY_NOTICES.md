## Stringer natural-image stimulus assets

- Path: `assets/natural_images/stringer_natimg2800_center_crop/`
- Source: Janelia Research Campus Figshare, [Recordings of 10,000 neurons in
  visual cortex in response to 2,800 natural
  images](https://janelia.figshare.com/articles/dataset/Recordings_of_ten_thousand_neurons_in_visual_cortex_in_response_to_2_800_natural_images/6845348)
- DOI: [10.25378/janelia.6845348](https://doi.org/10.25378/janelia.6845348)
- License: [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)

The 2,800 distributed grayscale PNGs were pixel-verified against the
Figshare `images_natimg2800_all.mat` source (published MD5
`4ccc523dffc948d3770dfa14bd51340f`). The frozen legacy conversion selects the
center 90 columns from each 68 × 270 source image and writes the resulting
68 × 90 `uint8` pixels as grayscale PNG, without resizing, resampling, rotation,
or transposition. The archive's extra top-level directory was removed during
extraction. See the asset [README](assets/natural_images/stringer_natimg2800_center_crop/README.md)
for conversion provenance and limitations.

The CC BY-NC 4.0 license applies to these assets, including the NonCommercial
restriction; the repository's BSD-3-Clause code license does not supersede it.
This project does not claim ownership of the source images.

(2019). High-dimensional geometry of population responses in visual cortex.
*Nature, 571*, 361–365. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1346-5>.
